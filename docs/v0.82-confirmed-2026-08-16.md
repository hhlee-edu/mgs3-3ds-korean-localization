## Follow-up 2 — the awkward wording is register drift, and it is systematic

The v0.81 mechanical shortening is **not** the cause: only 17 codec rows were
shortened, and they read acceptably (`quality-worklist.csv`, `kind=TONE`).

The real pattern is machine-translation register drift *within a single speaker's
continuous turn*. gcx 2181, resources 21-27 — one character telling one story:

| res | Korean | level |
|---:|---|---|
| 21 | 그러고 보니 생각나네. 내가 두 살 때 아버지가 … 들으셨어. | 반말 |
| 22 | 그것은 일요일 저녁 식사 직후였고, 우리는 … 취하고 있었습니다. | 존댓말 |
| 23 | … 나왔다고 하더군요. 실제 생방송 뉴스처럼 들렸습니다. | 존댓말 |
| 24 | 아버지는 … 당황하기 시작했다고 말했습니다. | 존댓말 |
| 26 | 그런데 아빠가 막 시동을 걸려고 할 때 … | 존댓말 |

Three defects visible in seven lines: the speech level flips at res 21→22; the same
person is `아버지` in res 24 and `아빠` in res 26; and res 22 opens `그것은 …`, a
literal rendering of "It was" that Korean does not use.

**Scale, measured** across the 8,141 accepted rows carrying a detectable ending:
2,404 polite, 3,142 casual, 41 internally mixed. 665 of 1,567 conversations contain
both levels — but that number is *not* a defect count, because a codec call has two
speakers and Snake speaking casually to a partner speaking politely is correct. The
data carries **no speaker field**, and vocative inference reaches only ~30% of rows,
so drift cannot be separated from normal two-speaker structure automatically. It has
to be read in conversation order, which is how the gcx 2181 case above was found.

### What that means for the fix

This is not a mechanical defect with a mechanical fix. It is a translation-quality
pass, and per the project rule text authoring goes to the translator rather than an
AI bulk rewrite — which is also what produced the drift in the first place.

What tooling can usefully contribute:

- a **conversation-ordered review export** (per GCX, resources in order, English
  beside Korean, sorted by `occurrences`) so register is judged in context;
- **MT-artifact flagging** as a first pass: sentence-initial `그것은`/`그것이`,
  mixed `아버지`/`아빠` style for one referent, `저`/`나` mixing inside one turn;
- per-GCX register statistics to target the worst conversations first.

None of that decides wording; it only puts the right lines in front of a person.
