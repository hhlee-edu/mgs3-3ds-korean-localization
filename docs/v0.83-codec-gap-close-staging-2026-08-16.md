### `gcx 414 res 11` — speaker determination

Shinsnote has no codec entry for this exchange, so the decision rests on the game
data. The English run is r10 `Wait a minute...` / r11 `What?` / r12 `You think I'd
drink alcohol in the middle of a mission?` / r13 `Wouldn't you?` / r14 `Hell no.` /
r15 `Well, I'm knocking a shot back now.` / r16 `What!?` / r17 `Just teasing you.`

r11 and r12 are one speaker's turn — *"What? You think I'd drink alcohol in the
middle of a mission?"* — and the party who is *on the mission* is Snake. The
master's own Korean agrees: r12 is already plain (`내가 임무 중에 술을 마실 것 같아?`),
as are r13/r14/r19/r20, while the two Para-Medic lines r15/r17 are polite. So r11
is Snake → **`뭐?`**, and r16 (between the two polite lines) is Snake → **`뭐!?`**.

**Separate defect found, not fixed here:** `414/15` is currently
`글쎄요, 이제 한 발 물러나겠습니다.`, which reads "I'll step back now" — a
mistranslation of *"I'm knocking a shot back now"* (she is having a drink). Out of
scope for this pass; logged for the translator queue.
