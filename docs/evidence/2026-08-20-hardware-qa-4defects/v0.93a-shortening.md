# v0.93a: the 40 held-back lines, resolved

Date: 2026-08-21. **Built, statically verified, staged. Not tested on hardware yet.**
No commit of build output, no tag, no push, no CCI.

## What the 40 lines were

Adopting the v0.93 `codec.dat` (PERSONAL DATA in clean English) removed the byte
subsidy the Korean PERSONAL DATA used to give its GCX records, so 40 unrelated
dialogue units across 31 records had to stay at source. This closes that.

## The 40 split three ways

| | count | treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| FR/ES donor slots | **9** | left at source permanently -- donor text is out of scope |
| shortened | **30** | Korean tightened so the record's byte pool closes |
| left unchanged | **1** | `1952:11`, see below |

The nine donor slots were confirmed by looking at their neighbours in clean
`codec.dat`, where the EN/ES/FR branches are laid out in sequence:

```
GCX   44: [14] EN  [15] ES  [16] FR
GCX 2153: [11] EN  [12][13] ES  [14][15] FR
GCX  847: [17][18][19] ES  [20][21][22] FR
GCX 1496: [20]..[24] all FR
GCX  433: [10] EN  [12] ES  [14] FR   ·  803:22 ES  ·  1869:15 ES  ·  1284:15 ES
```

Leaving a donor slot untranslated is itself the fix, and it alone closed **5 of
the 31 records** (44, 433, 1284, 1496, 1869). The remaining target was **236 B
across 26 records**, not the 290 B the original worklist implied.

## 1952:11 was deliberately NOT changed

`"스네이크"?` -> `Snake?` was approved, but that master row carries
**occurrences 591**: the edit would have restyled 591 locations across the whole
game, far outside a 40-line job. GCX 1952 closes without it -- `쥐입니다.` (7 B)
plus `...가이드가 나쁘지 않다더군요.` (12 B) covers the 16 B needed -- so the line
was left exactly as it was. Reverting this decision is a one-line change if the
591-location propagation is wanted.

## Shortening kept minimal

Proposals were tuned down from 459 B to **332 B secured against 236 B needed**:

```
margin per record:  0 B x4   1-3 B x12   4-8 B x8   39 B x1
```

The single outlier is GCX 290, and that is not over-shortening: the Korean there
had a second sentence about a balanced diet grafted onto a line whose English is
only about camouflage. Removing the stray sentence is a correction.
